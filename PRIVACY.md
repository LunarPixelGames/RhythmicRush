This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
For a better experience while using our Service, you may choose to create an online account. When you do, we require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information, including your email address and a chosen username. 

This information is used to:
* Provide cloud synchronization for your game progress and custom levels.
* Display your username on global leaderboards.
* Secure your account and verify your identity via email.

If you choose to play offline without an account, we do not require you to provide us with this personally identifiable information. 

However, the app does use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, primarily to serve advertisements. 

### 2. Online Services & Data Storage
We use third-party services to manage authentication and store your game data securely. When you create an account, your email, password, and authentication tokens are processed securely by **Firebase Authentication**. Your game progress, level data, and leaderboard scores are stored securely on our backend servers. 

### 3. Third-Party Services
Rhythmic Rush uses third-party advertising networks to show ads. These third parties may automatically collect certain information about your device, such as your IP address, device advertising ID (Google Play Services ID), network status, and general location (at the city/country level). This data is used solely to provide relevant advertisements and to measure their performance.

We use the following third-party service provider in our app:
* **[Google AdMob Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)**

### 4. Log Data
Whenever you use our Service, in the case of an error in the app, we collect data and information (through third-party products like Google Play) on your phone called Log Data. This Log Data may include information such as your device Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app when utilizing our Service, the time and date of your use of the Service, and other statistics. This is used strictly to fix bugs and improve game performance.

### 5. Children’s Privacy
Our Services are intended for a general audience and we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we will be able to take the necessary actions.
